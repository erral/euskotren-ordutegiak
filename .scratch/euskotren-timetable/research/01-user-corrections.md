## Resolved E1 names

- `Arroa` is in **Zestoa**, not Zumaia.
- The canonical stop name is **`Zazpikaleak/Casco Viejo`**.
- `Kukullaga-Etxebarri` and `Etxebarri` are **separate stops**; the E1 draft includes both.
- `Altzola` sits **between Mendaro and Toletxegain** and is a conditional stop (8:56, 12:56, 19:56, 21:56).
- `Traña Abadiño` is **one stop**, not two. The full-line table's single `TRAÑA ABADIÑO` column covers both names; remove the separate `Abadiño` stop.

## E1 service pattern

- E1 `Pasaia → Matiko` is a **through service**. The Pasaia–Amara Topo section is part of the E1 through service, not a branch; it overlaps E2 geographically.

## Shared stop naming

- The canonical stop is **`Amara Donostia`**. Merge E1's `AMARA / DONOSTIA` and E2's `DONOSTIA/SAN SEBASTIÁN AMARA` into it, following the other Donostia stops.
